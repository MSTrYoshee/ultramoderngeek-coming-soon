Photo Privacy Cleaner

Photos can carry embedded metadata — GPS coordinates, camera/phone model, timestamps, editing software, and more. This tool re-encodes the image in your browser to remove embedded metadata where present before you post it anywhere. Nothing is uploaded: the cleaning happens entirely on your device.
